# Sparq Platform Documentation

> **Complete feature documentation across three deployment levels**

## 📚 Documentation Structure

This documentation is organized into three progressive levels, each building upon the previous:

### [Level 1: Core Store](./LEVEL-1-CORE-STORE.md)
**The Foundation** - Everything needed to launch a professional online store

- ✅ Complete storefront with shopping cart
- ✅ Customer accounts and authentication  
- ✅ Admin dashboard for catalog management
- ✅ Order processing and tracking
- ✅ Payment integration (COD + mobile wallets)
- ✅ Dynamic branding and CMS
- ✅ Email notifications
- ✅ Reports and analytics

**Cost:** ৳3,600–7,200/month ($29–58)  
**Time to Launch:** 1-2 weeks  
**Perfect for:** Single-owner stores, 5-50 orders/day

---

### [Level 2: Growth Store](./LEVEL-2-GROWTH-STORE.md)
**Build Loyalty** - Add customer engagement and retention features

**Includes everything in Level 1, plus:**

- ✅ **Loyalty Points System** - Earn and redeem points
- ✅ **Membership Tiers** - Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond
- ✅ **Warranty Management** - Track coverage, file claims
- ✅ **Enhanced Dashboard** - Spending analytics, transaction history
- ✅ **Advanced Reports** - Customer lifetime value, cohort analysis
- ✅ **Email Campaigns** - Automated customer engagement
- ✅ **Notification Center** - Real-time activity feed

**Cost:** ৳7,200–14,400/month ($58–116)  
**Time to Upgrade:** 2-3 days  
**Perfect for:** Growing stores, 50-200 orders/day, focus on repeat customers

---

### [Level 3: Full Platform](./LEVEL-3-FULL-PLATFORM.md)
**Scale to Marketplace** - Multi-vendor platform with delivery management

**Includes everything in Level 2, plus:**

- ✅ **Multi-Vendor Marketplace** - Multiple sellers, one platform
- ✅ **Vendor Dashboard** - Complete seller workspace
- ✅ **Subscription Plans** - Free, Basic, Pro vendor tiers
- ✅ **Rider Delivery System** - In-house delivery fleet
- ✅ **Commission Management** - Automated revenue splits
- ✅ **Discount Coupons** - Store-wide + vendor-specific
- ✅ **Featured Promotions** - Paid product placements
- ✅ **Store Directory** - Public vendor discovery
- ✅ **Advanced Analytics** - Marketplace-wide metrics

**Cost:** ৳14,400–28,800/month ($116–232) + commission revenue  
**Time to Upgrade:** 1-2 weeks  
**Perfect for:** Marketplace businesses, 200-1,000+ orders/day

## 🎯 Choosing Your Level

### Start with Level 1 if:
- ✅ Just launching your online business
- ✅ Single owner/operator
- ✅ Limited budget (< ৳10K/month)
- ✅ Testing market demand
- ✅ Want professional store quickly

### Upgrade to Level 2 when:
- ✅ Consistent 50+ orders/day
- ✅ Ready to build customer loyalty
- ✅ Selling products with warranties
- ✅ Want deeper customer insights
- ✅ Focus on repeat business

### Upgrade to Level 3 when:
- ✅ Ready to become a marketplace
- ✅ Have operations team
- ✅ Want to onboard other sellers
- ✅ Can manage vendor relationships
- ✅ Targeting 200+ orders/day

## 📊 Migration Path

### Level 1 → Level 2

**What changes:**
- Database: Add loyalty and warranty collections
- Backend: Enable new endpoints
- Frontend: Activate hidden features
- No data loss, backward compatible

**Downtime:** None  
**Data migration:** Automatic  
**Time required:** 2-3 days configuration

### Level 2 → Level 3

**What changes:**
- Architecture: Multi-tenant support
- Database: Vendor and rider collections
- New dashboards: Vendor and rider portals
- Order processing: Split fulfillment

**Downtime:** Minimal (scheduled maintenance)  
**Data migration:** Requires planning  
**Time required:** 1-2 weeks full setup
